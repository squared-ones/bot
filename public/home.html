Moderation

Ban, kick, timeout, and purge with slash commands or straight from the dashboard — permission-checked and role-hierarchy safe.

Rules

Sensible defaults out of the box plus your own custom rules, auto-posted to a channel or DMed to new members on join.

Verification

Gate a "Verified" role behind a CAPTCHA and catch raids with account-age, default-avatar, and join-burst detection.

Tickets

Run a staff support desk with /ticket and a button panel, scoped to the category and staff role you choose.

Leveling

Reward activity with text and voice XP, level-up announcements, and a server leaderboard.

Discord OAuth

Sign in with Discord. Access is scoped to servers you actually belong to, so nothing leaks across servers.
