Default rules

Six sensible rules ship out of the box — respect, no spam, no NSFW, no ads, follow ToS, listen to mods. They can't be removed.

Custom rules

Add and remove your own rules via slash commands or the dashboard. Persisted to disk, so nothing gets lost.

Auto-posting

DM new members the rules the moment they join, or drop the full rules embed into any channel with /postrules.

Markdown formatting

Bold, italics, code, links, and blockquotes in rules and announcements.

Rule embeds

Publish consistent, readable rule embeds with /rules and /postrules.

Discord OAuth

Sign in with Discord. Channel access is scoped to servers you actually belong to, so nothing leaks across servers.
