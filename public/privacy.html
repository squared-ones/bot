Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

$ privacy --overview

Squared One ("the Service") is a Discord bot and optional web dashboard. This policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Squared One is designed to be self-hosted: the person or team running an instance ("the Operator") controls where it runs and the data it holds. This policy describes the default behavior of the software as distributed.

$ privacy --collect

Discord account information

When you sign in to the dashboard with Discord OAuth, the Service receives your Discord user ID, username, avatar, and the list of servers you belong to (the identify and guilds scopes). This is used only to authenticate you and to scope dashboard access to servers you belong to.

Server information

The bot caches basic server metadata from Discord — server names, icons, channel names, and member counts — to power the dashboard and its commands. This is standard Discord API data and is not used for any other purpose.

Rules you configure

Custom rules you add via slash commands or the dashboard are stored locally on the Operator's server in a rules.json file.

Messages

The bot reads recent messages only when a moderator runs /purge to delete them. Messages are not stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere by the Service.

Logs

The Service writes operational logs to the Operator's console (startup, connection, and error messages). Logs may contain Discord usernames or channel names referenced in errors, but are not shared with third parties.

$ privacy --use

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate dashboard users and scope access to their servers;
  • Provide the bot's features (rules, moderation, utility);
  • Operate and troubleshoot the Service.

The Service does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. It does not use your data for targeted advertising or profiling.

$ privacy --storage

Session cookies are HMAC-signed and httpOnly; they store only your Discord user ID, username, avatar, and the server IDs you belong to, and expire after 7 days. Discord OAuth access tokens are used transiently to fetch your profile and are not stored.

Custom rules persist in a local file until the Operator deletes them. Removing the bot from a server, or the Operator deleting its data directory, removes that data.

$ privacy --third-parties

The Service interacts with these third parties:

  • Discord — the Discord API and OAuth are used to operate the bot and dashboard. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to your Discord account.
  • Google Fonts — the dashboard loads the JetBrains Mono and Outfit fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Discord CDN — server and user avatars are loaded directly from Discord's content delivery network.

$ privacy --rights

You can:

  • Revoke the Service's access to your Discord account at any time via Discord's Authorized Apps settings;
  • Remove the bot from a server to stop it from reading that server's data;
  • Contact the Operator to request access to or deletion of data associated with you.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Discord's own Terms of Service require users to meet Discord's minimum age.

$ privacy --changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the Service after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

$ privacy --contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Operator of your instance. For the reference deployment, open an issue in the project repository.
